# Arrears and complaints

## Arrears
Contact the customer within 5 working days of a missed payment. Forbearance options include a temporary payment reduction of up to 6 months, a payment holiday of up to 3 months for mortgages, or a term extension. Fees must not be added during an agreed forbearance period.

## Complaints
Acknowledge complaints within 3 working days. Send a final response within 8 weeks. The final response must tell the customer they can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service within 6 months.

## Record keeping
Lending decisions, overrides and complaint outcomes are kept for 7 years after the account closes.
