# Residential mortgages

## Loan-to-value (LTV)
Maximum LTV is 90% for purchases and 85% for remortgages. Properties valued above £1,000,000 are capped at 75% LTV.

## Income multiples
Standard maximum lending is 4.5 times gross annual income. Up to 5 times is allowed only where gross household income is £75,000 or more and LTV is 85% or lower. No more than 15% of new mortgage lending by value in a quarter may exceed 4.5 times income.

## Stress test
Affordability must be assessed at the higher of the product rate plus 1 percentage point, or 7.0%.

## Property exclusions
Northbridge does not lend on ex-local-authority flats above the 6th floor, properties with less than 70 years remaining on the lease at the end of the mortgage term, or properties of non-standard construction without a structural engineer's report.
