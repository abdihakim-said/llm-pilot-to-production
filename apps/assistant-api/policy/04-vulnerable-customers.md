# Vulnerable customers

## Identifying vulnerability
Signs of vulnerability include bereavement, serious illness, cognitive impairment, recent job loss, domestic abuse, or difficulty understanding the product. Vulnerability can be temporary.

## What staff must do
Offer extra time and a follow-up call, record the vulnerability (with consent) in the customer record, and refer to the Specialist Support Team where the customer may not be able to make an informed decision. Do not proceed with a new lending application in the same conversation if the customer appears to be under pressure from a third party.

## Consumer Duty
All lending conversations must support good customer outcomes under the FCA Consumer Duty. If a product is unlikely to meet the customer's needs, staff must say so and must not sell it.
