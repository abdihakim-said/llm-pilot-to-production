# Credit checks and decisions

## Credit search
A soft search is used for eligibility quotes and does not affect the customer's credit file. A hard search is only performed once the customer submits a full application and has been told this will happen.

## Adverse credit
Applications are declined if the applicant has a County Court Judgment (CCJ) registered in the last 3 years, an active Individual Voluntary Arrangement (IVA), or has been declared bankrupt in the last 6 years. Defaults satisfied more than 2 years ago are referred, not automatically declined.

## Decline reasons
When an application is declined, staff must tell the customer which credit reference agency was used and that they can request a copy of their credit file. Staff must not speculate about other lenders' decisions.

## Overrides
Only a Lending Manager may override an automated decline, and must record the reason in the case notes within 1 working day.
