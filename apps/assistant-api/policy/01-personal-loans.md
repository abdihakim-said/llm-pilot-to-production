# Personal loans

## Eligibility
Applicants must be aged 18 or over, resident in the UK for at least 3 years, and have a Northbridge current account open for at least 3 months. Minimum gross annual income is £12,000.

## Loan amounts and terms
Personal loans range from £1,000 to £35,000. Terms range from 12 to 84 months. Loans above £25,000 require a second-level underwriter review.

## Affordability
Total monthly unsecured debt repayments, including the new loan, must not exceed 40% of net monthly income. Where income is variable (self-employed, commission, zero-hours), use the average of the last 2 years' tax calculations (SA302) or the last 6 months' payslips, whichever is lower.

## Purpose restrictions
Personal loans must not be used for gambling, investment in shares or crypto-assets, business purposes, or as a deposit for a property purchase.
